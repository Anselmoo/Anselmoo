Platform marks: third-party silhouettes (Simple Icons, CC0), flattened to the same grey and fenced in .pm. They name a language or a registry; they are not members of the drawn set.

Two files per icon, not a band pair. external.svg carries currentColor and takes the ink role of its context. external-mid.svg is for surface 1, where GitHub serves the file as an <img> and picks <picture> sources against the OS setting, not its own theme.

One band: the point of the midtone is that it does not need a second one, and the currentColor version has no colour of its own to show. 18-unit grid, 1-unit stroke, so at body size the stroke renders at 1.00px — the width of --hairline.

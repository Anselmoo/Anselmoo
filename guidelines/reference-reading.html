01 What was read

Nine portfolio references, adapted by admission test

The vocabulary of that genre is narrow and repeats: the name or the address at display scale, a numbered index of work, bare year-pairs, dated recognition rows, a place-and-time strip, section ordinals, and a marquee. Half of it this system already owned under another name. The other half collided with a measured rule, and a collision is the useful part.

05 What the strict version cost

Seven of the eight standing prohibitions were phrased only as refusals. A system made of refusals can state what a page must not do and has nothing to say about what it is for — which is how three earlier page drafts came out correct and empty. Shadows, badge rows, zebra striping and one unsourced headline figure are now in play. Italics are in play as a rule and absent as an asset: the cut is specified in FONT_CHAIN.sans.italic and not shipped, so emphasis is still weight 500 — the face and its token are gated together on the file existing, because a face with no file and a token with no face are the same dead declaration seen from two ends. No emoji is the one prohibition kept verbatim, and colour in typography was never dropped, which is why the new eye-separation is carried by five grounds rather than by hue.

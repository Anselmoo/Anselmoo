Layers3The series count: three greys and three geometries. A fourth layer would need a fourth geometry, not a fourth angle.
Depthink · paper · inkGrounds alternate: solid ink, a grey hatch on paper, a grey cross-hatch on ink. Every cut edge is light against dark, so the depth order survives a photocopy.
Inset · shadow48px · 12px, dy 4--space-7 per side per layer; the blur is one quarter of it, checked, so a shadow can never bridge two layers.
Fieldgrey, sampled stops--paper to --paper-dim, both pure greys. SVG interpolates in sRGB, so the path is sampled fine enough not to band.

The references for this were saturated purple, pink, green and orange. None of that is adoptable — this system carries no colour anywhere — but the construction is, and every parameter of it is decided elsewhere in the repository. The silhouette is the mark, scaled about its centre and placed point by point in canvas space, so the 4px hatch keeps its period instead of growing with the layer. The hero splits rather than overlays: text over imagery needs a scrim, a scrim is alpha, and the drop shadow is the single alpha value in this system — confined to the imagery layer and stated rather than discovered.

The column is not a taxonomy for its own sake: it is the promise a generator is allowed to make. It is also why the icon set stops at three — drawn classes ship as a system and a seed, and a complete icon library is a promise that cannot be kept. Absent is written out rather than left blank, because a table with a hole in it reads as an oversight.

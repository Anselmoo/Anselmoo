03 · The measure
03 The measure

No colour role was chosen. For every text role the lightness is solved by bisection against two contrast standards at once, on the most demanding of the five grounds. Fourteen intervals converge on the value in the token file.

This block is .prose--2col. A measure is a maximum, not a layout: 34em is the 75-character end of the 45-to-75 a line of prose wants, and stretching it to fill a 78rem sheet would break the rule the type scale rests on. So the width buys columns instead — and a column may only shrink to the floor of that range, 23em or 45.6 characters. Two of those need 860px of content box, three need 1306px, and below each threshold columns falls back on its own. The count is therefore what the container can afford and never a promise, which is why this paragraph does not tell you how many you are looking at.

.prose--3col, same floor. Three columns need 1306px of content box, so this shows three on a wide sheet and fewer on a narrow one without being told to.

Short paragraphs are what columns are for. A long one in a narrow column produces a tall thin block the eye has to climb, which is the opposite of the problem the measure solves.

break-inside: avoid keeps each paragraph whole, so a column boundary never lands mid-thought.

.prose--full drops the measure entirely — for one short line, never for prose.

The shadow under sheet 01 is bounded by this ladder: its darkest point may not darken its ground past dim, which caps it at alpha 0.078. In the dark band the grounds run the other way — paper is the darkest of the five — so there is nothing below it to bound a shadow against, and elevation rides the ground instead.

The paper

Generated, not sourced. Two frequency bands — fine grain plus low-frequency sheet formation — with the amplitude symmetric around the ground tone rather than below it, so the surface reads as material instead of turning grey. A grain overlay that only darkens lowers the mean luminance below the token value and pulls the already minimal warm chroma toward neutral.

Two repositories publish nothing and are counted as such rather than left out. werkstoff is a workshop for Claude Code plugins and dracula-palette computes colour harmonies from a seed hue; neither has a package, so neither has a usage figure, and both appear in the index with an em dash instead of a number.

Subjects

One construction per subject, each carrying the symmetry its own physics has rather than a symmetry imposed on it.

The subject is molecular electronic structure — specifically the mixing of ligand and transition-metal orbitals, which is covalency. Two atoms make a molecule; there is no atomistic spectroscopy. An earlier draft offered Bohr shells and a homonuclear σ/σ* pair: both withdrawn, the first for the framing and the second because a symmetric diatomic bond is not an unequal mixing. The covalency figure is an octahedral splitting where the barycentre rule does the work — 2(+0.6Δ) + 3(−0.4Δ) = 0, so it is balanced rather than mirrored, and the balance is the physics.

08 Refused, and what it cost

Nine portfolio references were read concept by concept. Five components were admitted. These were not, and the reasons are kept because a rule whose reason is lost gets re-broken.

Borrowed conceptDisposition
Marquee tickerAlready refused. .banner was admitted as the answer to a server-rendered banner that animated with no pause control.

Scroll-progress percentageThe rule is kept, the readout is not: scroll depth is neither reading progress nor time remaining.

Italic letters in the wordmarkCarried over to weight. 700 against 500, because three variable steps apart reads as a rendering fault. Prose italics were admitted as a rule, but the cut is not in the repository, so emphasis is weight alone.
Octocat and platform logosFenced into their own class. The drawn set admits a glyph only once its referent has been counted nineteen times; of six candidates the count admitted one.
Seven ink rolesRefused by the dark band at 1.190× against a floor of 1.20×.

What the strict version cost: seven prohibitions were phrased only as refusals, so the system could say what a page must not do and nothing about what it was for. Shadows, badges and zebra striping are now in play. Italics are in play as a rule and absent as an asset — the cut is specified, not shipped, so emphasis is still weight 500. Colour in typography is not in play at all: that one was kept, which is why the separation above is carried by grounds.
